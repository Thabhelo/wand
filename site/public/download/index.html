WAND FOR MAC

Wand for Mac.
Coming soon.

We’re building Wand so you can control your Mac from Muse. The Mac app is being tested. It isn’t ready for public download yet.

Public download: not released

Before you can download it, we need to finish account setup, connect it to Muse, and complete Apple’s security checks.

What works in the local preview?

Checking your Mac’s status, controlling music and volume, opening apps and websites, closing apps, and running Shortcuts you approve.

What’s still in progress?

Connecting to Muse, locking your Mac, putting it to sleep, closing browser tabs while keeping pinned tabs, and changing Focus settings.

System requirements

The current preview targets Apple silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. Windows and Linux are planned, not available downloads.
