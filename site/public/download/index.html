This build is not Apple-notarized. macOS may ask you to approve it before opening. Muse support is not connected yet; native remote pairing is still being verified.

Download Wand for Mac

How to install

  1. Download and open the disk image.
  2. Drag Wand into Applications, then open it.
  3. If macOS blocks it as an unidentified developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Find Wand and choose Open Anyway, then confirm. Only approve the app you downloaded here. Do not disable your Mac’s security protections.
  4. In Wand, open Connection. If prompted for Keychain access, enter your Mac login password in the macOS dialog and click Allow. Never enter it on a website.
  5. Click Connect and sign in with Google to link your Mac.

If you dismissed the Keychain dialog, click Allow Keychain access in Wand to request it again.

What works in the beta?

Checking your Mac’s status, controlling music and volume, opening apps and websites, closing apps, and running Shortcuts you approve.

What’s still in progress?

Connecting to Muse, locking your Mac, putting it to sleep, closing browser tabs while keeping pinned tabs, and changing Focus settings.

System requirements

The current preview targets Apple silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. Windows and Linux are planned, not available downloads.
