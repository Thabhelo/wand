Install

  1. Open the download and drag Wand into Applications. Updating? Quit Wand and replace it in its existing folder.
  2. Open Wand. This download is notarized by Apple.
  3. Click Connect and sign in with Google. Follow Wand’s permission setup.

If Keychain asks, choose Always Allow to remember access. Enter your password only in the macOS dialog.
