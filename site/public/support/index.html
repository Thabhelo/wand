Common questions

My Mac is offline

Make sure your Mac is awake, connected to the internet, and running Wand. Open Wand from its menu bar icon and check its connection. Then refresh Your Macs.

Your phone and Mac can be anywhere. They don’t need to share Wi-Fi.

I can’t sign in or find my Mac

Use the same sign-in account on your phone and Mac. If sign-in gets stuck, reload the page and choose Retry if it appears.

To connect a new Mac, open Wand on it, click Connect, sign in, and approve the connection. Only use the code under “Setup page didn’t open?” if automatic setup fails.

An action needs permission

Open Wand on your Mac. Enable desktop control if you want it to click, type, or scroll. macOS may ask you to allow Accessibility or Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

If a Keychain prompt identifies Wand, choose Always Allow to remember that choice. If the prompts keep returning, send us a message. Don’t uninstall Wand or reset all your permissions to troubleshoot.

Music or volume isn’t working

Open Music or Spotify on your Mac before using playback controls. Allow Wand’s Automation request on the Mac if one appears.

If the page says a volume reading is unavailable, your installed build may not support it yet. Check the download page for the current release.

An app won’t open or close

Choose Refresh apps, then select the app again. Use Other… if it isn’t listed. macOS background components cannot be opened or closed through these controls.

An app may stay open while it asks you to save a document. Check the Mac for an open dialog.

How do I connect Muse?

Muse access requires its connector registration and approval. Signing in to Wand doesn’t connect Muse automatically. You can use Wand’s web controls while that integration is being prepared.

I want to disconnect or delete my data

In Your Macs, select your Mac and choose Disconnect. To delete your Wand records and disconnect all your Macs, open Account settings → Delete my Wand data.
